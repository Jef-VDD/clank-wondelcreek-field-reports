### PROLOGUE

**Garji** from the Explorers Society needed the franchise to investigate disrupted ley lines, strange disappearances, and patches of dark magic cropping up across the region. Dran Enterprises had been hired for the same job — because apparently the franchise can't even get a monopoly on busywork.

**Bonkbeard** surprised everyone by volunteering to become the franchise's **Secretarian**, trading the hammer for a quill. Metaphorically. The hammer stayed within reach.

A letter from Portentia arrived, mocking the franchise's previous efforts and announcing that Dran Enterprises had expanded into the eastern territories. The Comfort Coaches now charged a fare. Everything was getting worse.

---

### FIELD OPERATIONS — DETAILED ACCOUNT

### THE DRAGON'S MISSION

Malathrex was furious this mission. Not the usual background menace — a sustained, relentless assault that turned every expedition into a sprint between cover. The franchise spent more time dodging green fire than investigating anything.

To make matters worse, Dran Enterprises operatives seized the least valuable artifact on the map — the very one **Sir Prize** had been heading toward. The warrior arrived to find an empty pedestal and boot prints leading east. Three missions of being outpaced by Piko, and now outpaced by Portentia's lackeys. The braids hung limp with defeat.

### THE VOLCANO

The whole franchise chipped away at the magic circle readings throughout the mission, but it was **Bonkbeard** who completed the final measurement — just in time for the ground beneath **Harry's** feet to split open. A volcano. An actual volcano, erupting directly under the wizard who already couldn't find the energy to move. The landscape reshaped itself around a very startled Harry, and the franchise gained one more reason to question the safety standards of this region.

Bonkbeard earned the **Associate Spotlight** for completing the readings, though the wizard standing in the blast zone may have had a different perspective on who deserved recognition.

### THE LONG CRAWL HOME

**Piko** made it home. Nobody else did. **Bonkbeard**, **Harry**, and **Sir Prize** each collapsed within sight of The Drunken Dragon — close enough to smell the pie, too battered to take another step. All three were knocked out above the danger line, earnings intact, dignity less so.

On the road back, **Portentia Dran** herself ambushed the franchise, demanding the research notes. After a brief scuffle, the associates escaped with most of their findings. Portentia had been too focused on the readings to steal anything else — a small mercy on a mission that offered precious few.

### AFTERMATH

The Explorers Society reviewed the franchise's findings — and its casualties. The readings were adequate. The artifacts recovered — Harry's silver bars and Sir Prize's helmet among them — were impressive enough to satisfy the Society's customers, and valuable enough to keep Dran Enterprises from claiming any of the remaining treasures on the board. A small victory. But the associates themselves? Too battered, too beaten down. Not a single one had enough left in them to earn the Society's official endorsement. Garji's disappointment was polite but unmistakable.

One thing was certain: the investigation had revealed that the real danger lay underground. The franchise would be heading into the depths next mission — the Underworld side of the region, where the ley lines converged and the answers waited. Assuming anyone was still standing by then.
