### PROLOGUE

The letter from the Home Office was in a different hand this time:

"Hi! Uh, is this thing on? Okay, hello! Hi. This is Jim Darkmagic. Yes, that Jim Darkmagic. I just heard about this Zrog Mogolth thing — an ancient, immortal, pandimensional horror who wants to wear your world as a hat or something? Seems like a jerk. Anyway, Omin filled me in on these shrines. Go find them. Good luck! (Was that good? Is it done? Quit. Stop. Goodbye!)"

The franchise descended into the Underworld with a patron who could not operate a letter.

### THE MORNING OF PAPERWORK

Before anyone went anywhere, there was the backlog. A pen had finally been located, and the field notes were brought up to date: the town, the miner, the smuggler, Barry's particulars, the author of the Zrog research — all of it entered into the record at last. The Home Office's insurance department exhaled.

Then the promotions. **Piko** collected a **Silver Associate** rank that had been sitting in the in-tray since last mission. And **Bonkbeard** had reached **Gold Associate**, rewarded for his must-win attitude. The dwarf read his letter in a corner, folded it, and said only that there was a sealed document involved. He did not elaborate. Mysterious.

Finally, an administrative correction. Owing to a cartographic discrepancy for a certain contract, the old vampire's ruin had been recorded on the wrong side of the world. By franchise decree the vampire was relocated to a more Underworldly one. He was not consulted.

### FIELD OPERATIONS — DETAILED ACCOUNT

### THE ORACLE

**Piko** found Madame Zoltar, a fortune teller in a rundown corner of a pyramid town, and a man in a cat mask waiting outside who promised that if the spirits liked the franchise, his friends would help too. The spirits spoke in verse:

> *In one of ten and one of three,*  
> *A circled door, but hard to see.*  
> *From there you seek; go four or two,*  
> *A long trip in comfort, or city streets through.*  
> *A gift of fruit ere you arrive*  
> *And seek her in one of boroughs five.*

The franchise read it, read it again, and argued about it for most of the mission.

### THE PICKPOCKET

Passing through a seedy district near the pyramid, Piko lifted a *single* coin from a stranger's pocket. Sir Prize had wanted to see what the black market artifact was. Sir Prize did not get to see.

### THE AMULET

Bonkbeard had carried Duke Destrin's amulet out of Mission 6; **Harry** carried it the last few steps. The old vampire's hand trembled as he took it. "I don't know if I will ever find forgiveness, but I can start making amends." He gave his name — **Saracula** — and dissolved into mist, and Acquisitions Incorporated gained a patron who had been waiting a very long time for someone to show up.

### CAELUM

Early, in the northeast, Harry noticed a flicker of light in the rock and chipped at it with a pickaxe until the wall gave way: a hidden tunnel, a starlit hallway, a portal. Beyond it, a stone platform in open blue sky — and sitting on it, as if forgotten, a very *very* valuable artifact. The wizard picked it up. The Shrine of Air was open, and every other associate had just seen what a shrine could pay.

### IGNIS

Sir Prize, still chasing the dwarf through the deep southwest, found a magic circle that opened a doorway of its own. Beyond it: wrought iron over a lava crater, heat like a forge, and one of Portentia's people already inside. The warrior dealt with the Dran agent without much ceremony. The Shrine of Fire was open.

### THE ORDER OF THE PINK CAT

Piko went deep — deeper than anyone — and found a stone fortress carved into the cavern wall, guarded by surprisingly amiable knights. The Order of the Pink Cat: sworn to stand at the gates of the world and hold them against invaders from beyond. They had felt Zrog Mogolth stirring. They welcomed another blade, and asked only that the franchise remember the name. The franchise wrote it down. There was a pen now.

### THE STILL LAKE

**Bonkbeard** had been eyeing a perfectly still underground lake all mission, had a plan involving a boat, and had said so — while **Sir Prize** shadowed him southwest, competing for everything the dwarf wanted. Harry simply went northeast instead, arrived at the lake first with exactly the right thing in his pack, and the water drew back into a tunnel along the lakebed. A coral platform. A granite pillar. A crash of water that did not drown him. The Shrine of Water was open, snooped out from under the dwarf's nose. Somewhere, the canoe wizard of Mission 1 nodded.

### THE RIDDLE, SOLVED

Three shrines open, and the verse still in Piko's pocket. The franchise sat down with it properly.

The route came first. "A long trip in comfort" — could these be Comfort Coaches? "One of ten and one of three": ten mushroom groves, yes, but three of *what*? Three magic circles — or three Dran encampments, which is where the franchise stared for a long while, at entirely the wrong patch of cavern.

And "boroughs five." Nobody could count five of anything. Ruins? Bonkbeard counted. Four.

Then the dwarf counted towns. Five. The groves, the circle, the coach, the two roads, the five towns — everything fit at once, and the only associate holding a mystic fruit was him.

He walked east to the spot...

He was right. A spirit with a fondness for fruit snapped its fingers and showed him a doorway in the eastern rock. Not the great artifact the dwarf had hoped for.

### UMBRA

Bonkbeard had already sprinted to the Shrine of Light earlier — standing since Mission 5 — to become the first associate carrying a shrine's blessing. **Associate Spotlight**. He had also, since then, watched Harry walk off a shrine platform with a free artifact.

So he went back down, marched to his new eastern doorway with the clear expectation that his shrine would come with a prize too, and stepped through into a void. Fallen stonework. Gnarled trees around a crypt. A faint wail from inside. Where Harry's shrine had handed over a treasure, Bonkbeard's handed over a ruin to dig through and something in it that did not want digging. The Shrine of Shadow was open. The dwarf was, for once, not impressed with his own luck. It would not improve.

### THE RECKONING

Five shrines stood open. The franchise should have gone home.

Instead, somewhere in the deepest part of the Underworld, the detour to the dark shrine did not prove fruitful for the dwarf. Bonkbeard was in trouble.

Seven missions of refusing to help, and the lesson had finally landed with his colleagues. Employees who don't make it up cannot be reviewed!

**Sir Prize** started buying. **Harry** quickly ran home making as much noise as possible. Even **Piko** bought the entire market in an afternoon. Every purchase rattled. Every rattle woke the dragon. Every time Malathrex looked down, he looked at the dwarf.

Bonkbeard went down in the depths, where the haul does not come home. There was cheering. It was not subtle. 

Then Sir Prize collapsed two steps from the inn, and Piko one step from it. Both above the danger line, hauls intact.

Harry walked in alone.

### THE SHRINE MAP

The epilogue found the franchise's wall covered in yarn, chalk, and five shrine locations. Light, Air, Water, Fire, Shadow. One missing. A squat clockwork automaton rolled in with a drink — officially branded, upgrades chosen by **Harry** as the franchise's resident fixer — and nobody asked where it had come from.

Two more letters arrived with it. **Sir Prize**: Silver Associate. **Harry**: Gold.

Which made two Gold Associates in one franchise — and made sense of Bonkbeard's morning. The sealed document in the dwarf's pocket needed two Gold seals broken together.

Harry read his letter. Looked at the dwarf. Looked at the letter again. "I'll think about it."
